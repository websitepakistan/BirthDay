The Letter
Happy Birthday, Baba.

You have spent your whole life making things easier for everyone else — and asking for nothing in return. Every quiet sacrifice, every late night, every small worry you carried alone so we wouldn't have to. We saw it, Baba. We see it still.

This page is a small thing, built with far less patience than you've shown us over the years, but every corner of it was made with the same intention you've always had for us: to make you feel loved, seen, and celebrated exactly as you deserve.

So on the 28th of August, we hope the day pauses for you the way you've always paused everything for us. No worries to carry, no one to look after but yourself — just a day that belongs entirely to you.

With all our love, always Your Family
